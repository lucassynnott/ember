# Ember: launch video plan

**What it is:** a private Mac app that writes up your calls, types what you say in any app, and keeps everything you copy and save. All on your Mac.
**For:** people who live on calls (founders, sales, operators) and want notes without a bot in the meeting or a cloud subscription.
**What sets it apart:** one app instead of six (Granola, Wispr Flow, Cluely, Snatch, a clipboard manager, a read-later app), and audio never leaves the Mac.
**Hook:** the Ember mark ignites, then "Your calls, written down." on black.
**Visual identity:** Ember's own: #161616 page, #1c1c1c cards, white-alpha hairlines, Geist, one ember-orange accent and the flame gradient (#ffc15e → #ff8a3d → #e8492c). Real app screens with fictional people (Alex Rivera, Priya Shah, Sam Okafor, Dana Lee).
**Tone:** polished: calm, premium, long enough holds to read, soft fades and gentle push-ins.
**Share caption:** Ember writes up your calls, types what you say, and keeps everything you copy, all on your Mac. Free, notarized, no account.

Effects in the same key and room: a breathy swell into the hook, soft air whooshes on cuts, a quiet key tick as text types, a low chime on the outro. Effects sit under the music.
