Local transcription, note generation, and storage.

Transcription

Speech from the Microphone device uses this name. Zoom system audio uses the detected participant name or the conservative Remote speaker fallback.

Live models transcribe while you talk. Whisper models transcribe after the recording ends. Downloads come from Fermion Research and Hugging Face and are checked against pinned SHA-256 hashes.

Zoom automation

Starts after Zoom exposes an active participant and stops after the meeting has remained closed for five seconds. Manual Stop pauses automation until that meeting ends.

Notion

After the local note is saved, the summary, action items and full transcript are added as a page in this Notion database using the Notion CLI (ntn). Failed saves are retried automatically.
